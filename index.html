What is Globalization?

Globalization is the process of increasing interconnectedness and interdependence of the world`s economies, culture, and politics.

Globalization is present in everyone it occurs in the daily life of everybody through communacation, interaction with eachother and it also affects the world state by maintaining peace in every country and fixes problems through political discussion and it is also vital in the world ecnomy because without interacting or any communacation with each other the world economy could collapse and it could bring catastrophic consequences.
